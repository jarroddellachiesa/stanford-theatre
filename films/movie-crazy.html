Movie Crazy

Director: Clyde Bruckman, Harold Lloyd

Producer: Harold Lloyd

Movie Crazy is a 1932 American Pre-Code comedy film starring Harold Lloyd in his third sound feature. The film's copyright was renewed in 1959.

Trivia

  • Clyde Bruckman, who had directed Lloyd in his first two talkie films along with the sound version of Speedy, was recruited to direct Lloyd, who also served as producer.
  • The film was a major box-office success.
  • An estimated $675,000 were spent on the production and the film grossed over $1,439,000 in the United States alone.
