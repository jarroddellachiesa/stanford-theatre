Susan Lenox: Her Fall and Rise

1931 ★ 76 min
Starring: Greta Garbo, Clark Gable, Jean Hersholt
Director: Robert Z. Leonard
Writer: Wanda Tuchock, Leon Gordon, Zelda Sears
Producer: Paul Bern

Susan Lenox (Her Fall and Rise) is a 1931 American pre-Code Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer film directed and produced by Robert Z. Leonard and starring Greta Garbo and Clark Gable. The film is based on a novel by David Graham Phillips. It is the only screen pairing of Garbo and Gable, who did not like each other. The notoriety of the novel alone caused British censors to ban the film's release.
