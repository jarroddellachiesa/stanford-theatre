Foreign Correspondent

1940 ★ 115 min

1940 film by Alfred Hitchcock

Foreign Correspondent is a 1940 American black-and-white spy thriller film directed by Alfred Hitchcock. It tells the story of an American reporter based in Britain who tries to expose enemy spies involved in a fictional continent-wide conspiracy in the prelude to World War II.

Trivia

  • Selected for the National Board of Review: Top Ten Films.
  • Nominated for: Academy Award for Best Picture; Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor; Academy Award for Best Cinematography, Black-and-White; Academy Award for Best Writing, Original Screenplay; Academy Award for Best Art Direction, Black and White; Academy Award for Best Special Effects.
