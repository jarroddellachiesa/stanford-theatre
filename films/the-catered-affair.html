The article is about the film. For the stage adaptation, see A Catered Affair. The Catered Affair (also known as Wedding Party) is a 1956 American comedy drama film directed by Richard Brooks and produced by Sam Zimbalist from a screenplay by Gore Vidal, based on a 1955 television play from Goodyear Television Playhouse by Paddy Chayefsky.

Trivia

  • Selected for the National Board of Review: Top Ten Films.
  • MGM bought the screen rights to the 1955 TV play that aired on Goodyear Television Playhouse in 1955.
  • Ann Blyth was originally announced for the female lead Jane Hurley.
  • In 1964, Debbie Reynolds said she "hated making" the film "for personal reasons.
