The Secret Garden

1949 ★ 92 min
Starring: Margaret O'Brien, Herbert Marshall, Dean Stockwell
Director: Fred M. Wilcox
Writer: Robert Ardrey
Producer: Clarence Brown

The Secret Garden is a 1949 American drama film directed by Fred M. Wilcox and produced by Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer (MGM). Adapted from the classic 1911 novel by Frances Hodgson Burnett, The Secret Garden is the second film adaptation following a silent version released in 1919. The film stars Margaret O'Brien, Herbert Marshall, Dean Stockwell, Gladys Cooper, and Elsa Lanchester.

Trivia

  • Based on The Secret Garden (1912 novel by Frances Hodgson Burnett).
  • MGM announced the film in November 1946.
