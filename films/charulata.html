Charulata

1964 ★ 117 min
Starring: Soumitra Chatterjee, Madhabi Mukherjee, Bankim Ghosh
Director: Satyajit Ray
Writer: Satyajit Ray

Charulata (Bengali: চারুলতা, [t͡ʃaɾulɔt̪a] ; also known as The Lonely Wife) is a 1964 Indian drama film written and directed by Satyajit Ray. Based on Rabindranath Tagore's novella Nastanirh, it stars Soumitra Chatterjee, Madhabi Mukherjee, and Shailen Mukherjee. The film is widely regarded as one of Ray's finest works. Both the opening and closing scenes of the film have received critical acclaim.

Trivia

  • Won the National Film Award for Best Feature Film.
  • Won the Silver Bear for Best Director.
  • Based on Nastanirh (1901 Bengali novella by Rabindranath Tagore).
  • Charulata is based on the 1901 novella Nastanirh (The Broken Nest) by Bengali author Rabindranath Tagore.
  • Ray later said that he liked the novella because "it has a western quality to it and the film obviously shares that quality.
  • Ray spent many months researching the film's historical setting, working for the first time in his career without a deadline for either pre-production or the shoot.
