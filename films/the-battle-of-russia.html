The Battle of Russia

1943 ★ 83 min
Studio: United States Department of War, United States Office of War Information
Director: Frank Capra, Anatole Litvak
Writer: Julius J. Epstein, Philip G. Epstein, Anthony Veiller

The Battle of Russia (1943) is the fifth film of Frank Capra's Why We Fight documentary series. The longest film of the series, it has two parts. It was made in collaboration with Russian-born Anatole Litvak as primary director under Capra's supervision. Litvak gave the film its "shape and orientation," and the film had seven writers with voice narration by Walter Huston.

Trivia

  • Nominated for: Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature Film.
