The Wind

1928 ★ 95 min
Starring: Lillian Gish, Lars Hanson, Montagu Love
Director: Victor Sjöström
Writer: Frances Marion
Producer: Irving Thalberg

The Wind is a 1928 American synchronized sound romantic drama film directed by Victor Sjöström. While the film has no audible dialog, it was released with a synchronized musical score with sound effects using both the sound-on-disc and sound-on-film process. The movie was adapted by Frances Marion from the 1925 novel of the same name written by Dorothy Scarborough.

Trivia

  • Based on The Wind (novel by Dorothy Scarborough).
  • Gish came up with the idea of making a film adaptation of the novel of the same name.
  • Irving Thalberg immediately gave her permission to do so.
  • Gish recalled wanting Lars Hanson as her leading man after seeing him in a Swedish film with Greta Garbo.
