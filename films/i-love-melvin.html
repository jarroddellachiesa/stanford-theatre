I Love Melvin

1953 ★ 77 min
Starring: Donald O'Connor, Debbie Reynolds, Una Merkel
Director: Don Weis
Writer: H. G. Wells
Producer: George Wells

I Love Melvin is a 1953 American Technicolor musical romantic comedy film directed by Don Weis, starring Donald O'Connor and Debbie Reynolds. The film's most famous scene depicts Reynolds playing a human American football in a dance sequence. The film reunited O'Connor and Reynolds after 1952's Singin' in the Rain.
