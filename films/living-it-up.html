Living It Up

1954 ★ 95 min
Starring: Dean Martin, Jerry Lewis, Janet Leigh
Director: Norman Taurog
Writer: Jack Rose, Melville Shavelson, Ben Hecht
Producer: Paul Jones

Living It Up is a 1954 American comedy film starring Dean Martin and Jerry Lewis which was released by Paramount Pictures. The film was directed by Norman Taurog and produced by Paul Jones. The screenplay by Jack Rose and Melville Shavelson was based on the musical Hazel Flagg (1953) by Ben Hecht, which was in turn based on the story "Letter to the Editor" by James H. Street.

Trivia

  • Sig Ruman, who plays Dr. Emil Eggelhoffer, had played the same role in the 1937 version.
  • Doing a jitterbug in a dance scene with Lewis, actress Sheree North's character is introduced by a master of ceremonies with her own name, "Sheree North." The song "Every Street's a Boulevard in Old New York", performed by Martin and Lewis, was originally written by Jule Styne for the musical Hazel Flagg.
  • Martin also sings Styne's songs How Do You Speak to an Angel? and Money Burns a Hole in My Pocket in this film.
