The White Sister

1923 ★ 143 min
Studio: Inspiration Pictures
Starring: Lillian Gish, Ronald Colman, Charles Lane
Director: Henry King
Writer: Don Bartlett
Producer: Henry King

The White Sister is a 1923 American silent drama film starring Lillian Gish and Ronald Colman, directed by Henry King, and belatedly released by Metro Pictures. It was based on the 1909 play of the same name by Francis Marion Crawford and Walter Hackett.

Watch Online: Internet Archive

Trivia

  • Gish turned down a weekly salary of $3500 from Tiffany Pictures and instead signed with Inspiration Pictures for $1500 and a share of the profits.
  • Inspiration had purchased the rights to the novel The White Sister for $16,000.
  • Gish involved herself in all aspects of the production.

Filming Locations

  • Filming took place in studios in Rome and outdoor locations in Rome and Naples, with the desert scenes shot in Algeria.
