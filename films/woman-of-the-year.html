Woman of the Year

1942 ★ 113 min
Starring: Spencer Tracy, Katharine Hepburn, Fay Bainter
Director: George Stevens
Writer: Ring Lardner Jr., Michael Kanin, John Lee Mahin
Producer: Joseph L. Mankiewicz

Woman of the Year is a 1942 American romantic comedy drama film directed by George Stevens and starring Spencer Tracy and Katharine Hepburn. The film was written by Ring Lardner Jr. and Michael Kanin (with uncredited work on the rewritten ending by John Lee Mahin), and produced by Joseph L. Mankiewicz.

Trivia

  • Won the Academy Award for Best Writing, Original Screenplay.
  • Also nominated for: Academy Award for Best Actress.
  • The outline for the film was developed by Garson Kanin, a close friend of Hepburn's. The character of Tess Harding was based on Dorothy Thompson, an American journalist and radio broadcaster who was highly influential.
  • Hepburn then passed the outline to Joseph L. Mankiewicz at MGM, and said the price was $250,000 – half for her, half for the script.
  • Kanin was fighting in the war at the time, so the script was written by his brother, Michael Kanin, and mutual friend Ring Lardner Jr. Hepburn contributed significantly to the script – reading it, suggesting cuts and word changes, and generally providing helpful enthusiasm for the project.
