Jalsaghar (Bengali: জলসাঘর Jalsāghar, lit. 'The Music Room') is a 1958 Indian Bengali drama film written and directed by Satyajit Ray, based on a popular short story by Bengali writer Tarasankar Bandyopadhyay, and starring Chhabi Biswas. The fourth of Ray's feature films, it was filmed at Nimtita Raajbari in Nimtita, Murshidabad district.

Trivia

  • Won the National Film Award for Second Best Feature Film.
  • Won the National Film Award for Best Bengali Feature Film.
  • Jalsaghar was based on a popular short story written by Bengali writer Tarasankar Bandyopadhyay.
  • After the box office failure of Aparajito (1956), Ray desperately needed a hit, and he decided to make a film that both was based on a popular piece of literature and would incorporate Indian music.
  • Production on the film began in May 1957.
