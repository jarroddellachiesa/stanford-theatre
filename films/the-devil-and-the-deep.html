The Devil and the Deep

1932 ★ 78 min
Starring: Tallulah Bankhead, Gary Cooper, Charles Laughton
Director: Marion Gering
Writer: Benn Levy

Devil and the Deep is a 1932 American pre-Code drama film directed by Marion Gering, based on Maurice Larrouy's novel (Sirenes et Tritons), and starring Tallulah Bankhead, Gary Cooper, Charles Laughton and Cary Grant. It follows a naval commander who has alienated his wife due to his insane jealousy over every man she speaks to.
