Mystery of the Wax Museum

1933 ★ 77 min
Studio: Vitaphone
Starring: Lionel Atwill, Fay Wray, Glenda Farrell
Director: Michael Curtiz
Producer: Henry Blanke

Mystery of the Wax Museum is a 1933 American pre-Code mystery-horror film directed by Michael Curtiz and starring Lionel Atwill, Fay Wray, Glenda Farrell, and Frank McHugh. It was produced and released by Warner Bros. Pictures and filmed in two-color Technicolor; Doctor X and Mystery of the Wax Museum were the last two dramatic fiction films made using this process.

Trivia

  • The film is based on an unpublished short story, "The Wax Works", by Charles S. Belden, who had also written a play called The Wax Museum, which was optioned by Charles Rogers, an independent producer.
  • Rogers dropped his option on the play when the co-author of a Broadway play with a similar plot threatened him with a lawsuit.
  • A follow-up to Warner's earlier horror film Doctor X (1932), Mystery involved many of the same cast and crew, including actors Lionel Atwill, Fay Wray, Arthur Edmund Carewe, and Thomas Jackson; director Michael Curtiz; art director Anton Grot; and cameraman Ray Rennahan.
