These Glamour Girls

1939 ★ 79 min
Starring: Lew Ayres, Lana Turner, Tom Brown
Director: S. Sylvan Simon
Writer: Jane Hall
Producer: Sam Zimbalist

These Glamour Girls is a 1939 American screwball comedy drama film directed by S. Sylvan Simon and starring Lew Ayres and Lana Turner. A satire on social class, the film follows an uppercrust male college student (Ayres) who drunkenly invites a taxi dancer (Turner) to spend the weekend with him at his snobbish college. It is based on a story of the same name by Jane Hall, originally published in Cosmopolitan magazine.

Trivia

  • Hall based numerous elements of the screenplay on the social life of Princeton University students.
  • Film censors expressed some hesitancy over the screenplay due to its depiction of youth alcohol consumption, implied sexual activity, suicide, and an "unflattering" depiction of U.S. sailors engaging in a brawl.
  • Lew Ayres was given top-billing in the production as Philip S. Griswold, while a teenage Lana Turner was cast in the role of Jane.
