Odd Man Out

1947 ★ 111 min
Studio: Two Cities Films
Starring: James Mason, Robert Newton, Cyril Cusack
Director: Carol Reed
Writer: R. C. Sherriff
Producer: Carol Reed

Odd Man Out is a 1947 British film noir directed by Carol Reed, and starring James Mason, Robert Newton, Cyril Cusack, and Kathleen Ryan. Set in Belfast, Northern Ireland, it follows a wounded Nationalist leader who attempts to evade police in the aftermath of a robbery. It is based on the 1945 novel of the same name by F. L. Green.

Trivia

  • Selected for the National Board of Review: Top Ten Films.
  • Won the BAFTA Award for Best British Film.
  • Also nominated for: Academy Award for Best Film Editing.
  • Based on Odd Man Out (1945 novel).
  • F.L. Green's novel, also used as the basis of the 1969 Sidney Poitier film The Lost Man, was published in 1945.
  • In the novel, an IRA plot goes horribly wrong when its leader, Johnny Murtah, kills an innocent man, and he is gravely wounded.
  • The source of Green's familiarity with the Belfast IRA at the time is thought to be the Belfast writer Denis Ireland.

Filming Locations

  • Exterior scenes were shot in West Belfast, although some were shot at Broadway Market in London.
