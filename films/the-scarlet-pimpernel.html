The Scarlet Pimpernel is a 1934 British adventure film starring Leslie Howard, Merle Oberon, and Raymond Massey. It was directed by Harold Young and produced by Alexander Korda.

Trivia

  • Based on The Scarlet Pimpernel (novel by Emma Orczy).
  • By 1934, Alexander Korda was attempting to elevate the British film industry to the perceived level of the American film industry.
  • To do so, he decided to import American directors to work on his films, inviting (among others) image-oriented writer-director Rowland Brown to direct The Scarlet Pimpernel—a huge opportunity for the young director.
  • Korda, a Hungarian born in a town not far from Pimpernel authoress Baroness Orczy's farm, had recently had great success with the actor Charles Laughton in the film The Private Life of Henry VIII, so he asked Laughton to play the role of Sir Percy.
