The Barkleys of Broadway

1950 ★ 109 min
Starring: Fred Astaire, Ginger Rogers, Oscar Levant
Director: Charles Walters
Writer: Sidney Sheldon, Betty Comden, Adolph Green
Producer: Arthur Freed, Roger Edens

The Barkleys of Broadway is a 1949 American Technicolor musical comedy film produced by Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer (MGM), which reunited Fred Astaire and Ginger Rogers after ten years apart. Directed by Charles Walters, the screenplay was written by Betty Comden, Adolph Green, and Sidney Sheldon.

Trivia

  • Nominated for: Academy Award for Best Cinematography, Color.
  • The Barkleys of Broadway was conceived under the working title You Made Me Love You, with Judy Garland and Fred Astaire intended to reunite after Easter Parade (1948).
  • Even before the film's release, in March 1948, screenwriters Betty Comden and Adolph Green delivered an original script about Josh and Dinah Barkley, a successful Broadway husband-and-wife team, whose careers split when Dinah decides to become a dramatic actress.
  • Roger Edens suggested the title be changed to The Barkleys of Broadway.
