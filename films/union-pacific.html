Union Pacific

1939 ★ 135 min
Starring: Barbara Stanwyck, Joel McCrea, Akim Tamiroff
Director: Cecil B. DeMille
Writer: C. Gardner Sullivan, Walter DeLeon, Jesse Louis Lasky Jr.
Producer: Cecil B. DeMille

Union Pacific is a 1939 American Western drama directed by Cecil B. DeMille and starring Barbara Stanwyck, Joel McCrea and Robert Preston. Based on the 1936 novel Trouble Shooter by Western fiction author Ernest Haycox, the film is about the building of the Union Pacific Railroad across the American West.

Trivia

  • Nominated for: Academy Award for Best Special Effects.
  • Union Pacific is a celebration of American industry.
  • DeMille recognized that the time was right for "chauvinistic fairy tales". The film is essentially a remake of John Ford's The Iron Horse.
  • DeMille even copies several of Ford's shots, especially during the attack on the supply train.

Filming Locations

  • Parts of the film were shot in Iron Springs, Utah.
