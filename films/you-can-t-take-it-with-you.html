You Can't Take It with You

1938 ★ 126 min
Starring: Jean Arthur, Lionel Barrymore, James Stewart
Director: Frank Capra
Writer: Robert Riskin
Producer: Frank Capra

You Can't Take It with You is a 1938 American screwball romantic comedy film directed by Frank Capra, and starring Jean Arthur, Lionel Barrymore, James Stewart, and Edward Arnold.

Trivia

  • Won the Academy Award for Best Picture.
  • Won the Academy Award for Best Director.
  • Also nominated for: Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress; Academy Award for Best Cinematography; Academy Award for Best Film Editing; Academy Award for Best Writing, Adapted Screenplay; and 1 more.
  • In 1937, Harry Cohn of Columbia Pictures bought the film rights of the original play for $200,000 ($3,589,000 in 2019).
  • In reality, it was due to his increasing arthritis – earlier in the year he had been forced to withdraw from the movie A Christmas Carol.
