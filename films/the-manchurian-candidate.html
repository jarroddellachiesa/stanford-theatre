The Manchurian Candidate

1962 ★ 126 min
Starring: Frank Sinatra, Janet Leigh, Angela Lansbury
Director: John Frankenheimer
Writer: George Axelrod
Producer: John Frankenheimer, George Axelrod

The Manchurian Candidate is a 1962 American neo-noir political thriller film directed and produced by John Frankenheimer. The screenplay is by George Axelrod, based on the 1959 Richard Condon novel The Manchurian Candidate. The film's leading actors are Frank Sinatra, Laurence Harvey, and Janet Leigh, with co-stars Angela Lansbury, Henry Silva, and James Gregory.

Trivia

  • Nominated for: Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress; Academy Award for Best Film Editing.
  • Based on The Manchurian Candidate (novel by Richard Condon).
  • George Axelrod was friends with John Frankenheimer and they were looking for a project to make after Frankeheimer had been removed as director of Breakfast at Tiffany's. Axelrod read a review of The Manchurian Candidate in the New Yorker, bought the book and he and Frankenheimer decided to turn it into a movie.
