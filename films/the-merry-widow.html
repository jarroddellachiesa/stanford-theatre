The Merry Widow

1934 ★ 99 min
Starring: Maurice Chevalier, Jeanette MacDonald, Edward Everett Horton
Director: Ernst Lubitsch
Writer: Marcel Achard, Ernst Lubitsch, Ernest Vajda
Producer: Irving Thalberg, Ernst Lubitsch
Studio: Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer

The Merry Widow is a 1934 film adaptation of the 1905 operetta of the same name by Franz Lehár. The film was directed and produced by Ernst Lubitsch and stars Maurice Chevalier and Jeanette MacDonald. A French-language version was produced at the same time and released in France the same year as La veuve joyeuse.

Trivia

  • Won the Academy Award for Best Production Design.
  • Curtis Bernhardt, who directed the 1952 version of The Merry Widow, maintained that because the script for the 1934 film lacked a third act, Lubitsch adapted the jail scene from the third act of the Johann Strauss II comic operetta Die Fledermaus.
