Bachelor Mother

1939 ★ 83 min
Starring: Ginger Rogers, David Niven, Charles Coburn
Director: Garson Kanin
Writer: Norman Krasna
Producer: Buddy DeSylva

Bachelor Mother is a 1939 American romantic comedy film directed by Garson Kanin and starring Ginger Rogers, David Niven, and Charles Coburn. The screenplay was written by Norman Krasna from an Academy Award-nominated story by Felix Jackson (a.k.a. Felix Joachimson) written for the 1935 Austrian-Hungarian film Little Mother.

Trivia

  • Nominated for: Academy Award for Best Story.
  • The film was a remake of the 1935 Hungarian film Little Mother from Joe Pasternak and Henry Koster which was never screened in the United States.
  • In November 1938, RKO announced that Little Mother would star Ginger Rogers.
  • The film replaced Perfect Honeymoon and She Married for Money in Rogers' schedule at RKO. Louis Hayward was originally announced as the male lead.
