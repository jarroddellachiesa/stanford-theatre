Waterloo Bridge

1940 ★ 108 min
Starring: Robert Taylor, Vivien Leigh, Lucile Watson
Director: Mervyn LeRoy
Writer: Robert E. Sherwood, S. N. Behrman, Hans Rameau
Producer: Sidney Franklin

Waterloo Bridge is a 1940 American drama film as well as the remake of the 1931 pre-Code film of the same name, adapted by S. N. Behrman, Hans Rameau and George Froeschel, from the 1930 play Waterloo Bridge by Robert E. Sherwood. In an extended flashback narration, it recounts the story of a dancer and an army captain who meet by chance on Waterloo Bridge in London.

Trivia

  • Nominated for: Academy Award for Best Cinematography, Black-and-White; Academy Award for Best Original Score.
  • Based on Waterloo Bridge (play by Robert E. Sherwood).
  • MGM bought the 1931 version of Waterloo Bridge from Universal Studios in order to produce the remake.
  • From its initial release, the 1931 film had been censored in major American cities because of objections to the portrayal of prostitution.
  • The censorship was imposed first by local authorities, such as a Chicago police censorship board, before enforcement by the Hays Office in 1934 made it impossible to exhibit the film anywhere in the United States.
