Platinum Blonde

1931 ★ 89 min
Starring: Loretta Young, Robert Williams, Jean Harlow
Director: Frank Capra
Writer: Robert Riskin, Jo Swerling, Harry Chandlee
Producer: Harry Cohn

Platinum Blonde is a 1931 American pre-Code romantic comedy film directed by Frank Capra, written by Jo Swerling, and starring Loretta Young, Robert Williams, and Jean Harlow. It was Williams's last screen appearance; he died of peritonitis three days after the film's October 31 release. Though not as well known as Capra's later 1930s movies, the film's reputation has grown over the years.
