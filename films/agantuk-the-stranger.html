Agantuk (The Stranger)

1991 ★ 115 min
Studio: National Film Development Corporation of India
Starring: Utpal Dutt, Deepankar De, Mamata Shankar
Director: Satyajit Ray
Writer: Satyajit Ray
Producer: Satyajit Ray

Agantuk (lit. 'Guest'; known by its English title The Stranger) is a 1991 Bengali-language drama film written and directed by Satyajit Ray. Notable for being Ray's last film, it was based on one of his own short stories, Atithi. A joint Indo-French production, it received financial backing from companies such as Gérard Depardieu's DD Productions and Canal+.

Trivia

  • Won the National Film Award for Best Feature Film.
  • The film was a joint Indian-French production with financial backing from Gérard Depardieu's DD Productions, Canal+ and a couple of other French companies.
  • Depardieu is credited as executive producer.
