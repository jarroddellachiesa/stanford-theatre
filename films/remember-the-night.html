Remember the Night

1939 ★ 94 min
Starring: Barbara Stanwyck, Fred MacMurray, Beulah Bondi
Director: Mitchell Leisen
Writer: Preston Sturges
Producer: Mitchell Leisen, Albert Lewis

Remember the Night is a 1940 American Christmas romantic comedy trial film directed by Mitchell Leisen and starring Barbara Stanwyck and Fred MacMurray. The film was written by Preston Sturges and was the last of his scripts shot by another director, as Sturges began his own directorial career the same year with The Great McGinty.

Trivia

  • Preston Sturges suggested Great Love as a title for the film.
  • Director Mitchell Leisen shortened Sturges' script considerably, both before and during shooting.
  • Leisen's script alterations shifted the film's focus from MacMurray's character to that of Stanwyck.
