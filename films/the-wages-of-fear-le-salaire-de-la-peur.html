Studio: Vera Films

Writer: Henri-Georges Clouzot, Georges-Jean Arnaud
Producer: Raymond Borderie, Henri-Georges Clouzot

The Wages of Fear (French: Le Salaire de la peur) is a 1953 thriller film directed, co-written, and co-produced by Henri-Georges Clouzot, adapted from Georges Arnaud's 1950 novel of the same name, and starring Yves Montand, Charles Vanel, Peter van Eyck and Véra Clouzot.

Trivia

  • Won the Golden Bear.
  • Won the Palme d'Or.
  • Won the BAFTA Award for Best Film.
  • Based on Le Salaire de la peur (French novel by Georges Arnaud (1949)).
  • Jean Gabin declined the role of Jo, feeling such a cowardly character would hurt his career.
  • Clouzot originally wanted to film on-location in Guatemala, but budgetary limitations confined shooting to continental Europe.
  • Montand flatly rejected proposals to film in Almería, Spain, due to his opposition to the Francoist regime.

Filming Locations

  • Ultimately, filming took place mainly in the Gard department of Occitania.
  • A lengthy section of the film was shot in the Bambouseraie de Prafrance, due to its exotic vegetation.
