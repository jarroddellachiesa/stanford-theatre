Friendly Persuasion is a 1956 American Civil War drama film produced and directed by William Wyler. It stars Gary Cooper, Dorothy McGuire, Anthony Perkins, Richard Eyer, Robert Middleton, Phyllis Love, Mark Richman, Walter Catlett and Marjorie Main. The screenplay by Michael Wilson was adapted from the 1945 novel The Friendly Persuasion by Jessamyn West.

Trivia

  • Won the Palme d'Or.
  • Selected for the National Board of Review: Top Ten Films.
  • Also nominated for: Academy Award for Best Picture; Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor; Academy Award for Best Director; Academy Award for Best Writing, Adapted Screenplay; and 2 more.
  • Based on The Friendly Persuasion (1945 novel by Jessamyn West).
  • The film was in development for eight years; producer-director William Wyler brought the project to Allied Artists Pictures Corporation (formerly known as Monogram Pictures Corporation) from Paramount; Allied agreed to a $1.5 million budget for what was Wyler's first film in color for a commercial studio.
  • The film's shooting location was moved from southern Indiana to a combination of a Republic studio and a San Fernando Valley estate.
  • The film ended up costing over $3 million.
