Secrets

1933 ★ 90 min
Starring: Mary Pickford, Leslie Howard, C. Aubrey Smith
Director: Frank Borzage
Writer: Frances Marion
Producer: Mary Pickford

Secrets is a 1933 American pre-Code Western film directed by Frank Borzage and starring Mary Pickford in her last film role. The film is a remake of Secrets (1924), a silent film starring Norma Talmadge, which was based on a 1922 play of the same name. In 1930, Pickford had begun a remake of the Norma Talmadge Secrets titled Forever Yours with director Marshall Neilan and actors Kenneth MacKenna and Don Alvarado.
