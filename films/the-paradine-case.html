The Paradine Case

1947 ★ 125 min

Director: Alfred Hitchcock
Writer: Alma Reville, James Bridie, Ben Hecht
Producer: David O. Selznick

The Paradine Case is a 1947 American courtroom drama film with elements of film noir set in England, directed by Alfred Hitchcock and produced by David O. Selznick. Selznick and an uncredited Ben Hecht wrote the screenplay from an adaptation by Alma Reville and James Bridie of the 1933 novel by Robert Smythe Hichens.
