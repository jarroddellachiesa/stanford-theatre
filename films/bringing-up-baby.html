Bringing Up Baby

1938 ★ 102 min
Starring: Katharine Hepburn, Cary Grant, Charles Ruggles
Director: Howard Hawks
Writer: Dudley Nichols, Hagar Wilde
Producer: Howard Hawks, Cliff Reid

Bringing Up Baby is a 1938 American screwball comedy film directed by Howard Hawks, and starring Katharine Hepburn and Cary Grant. It was released by RKO Radio Pictures. The film tells the story of a paleontologist in a number of predicaments involving a scatterbrained heiress and a leopard named Baby.

Trivia

  • In March 1937 Howard Hawks signed a contract at RKO for an adaptation of Rudyard Kipling's Gunga Din, which had been in pre-production since the previous fall.
  • In April he read a short story by Hagar Wilde in Collier's magazine called Bringing Up Baby and immediately wanted to make a film from it, as it had made him laugh out loud.
  • RKO bought the screen rights in June for $1,004, and Hawks worked briefly with Wilde on the film's treatment.
