The Private Life of Sherlock Holmes is a 1970 DeLuxe Color film in Panavision written and produced by Billy Wilder and I. A. L. Diamond, and directed by Wilder.

Trivia

  • Billy Wilder, a long time Holmesian, had long aspired to create a musical adaptation of the Sherlock Holmes stories.
  • After two failed attempts in 1955 and 1963, he decided to embark on a non-musical screenplay with collaborator I. A. L. Diamond.
  • Initially, Wilder planned to cast Peter O'Toole as Holmes and Peter Sellers as Watson.

Filming Locations

  • Pinewood Studios
  • Elaborate sets were built on the backlot at Pinewood Studios, including 150 yards of the Baker Street set, at a cost of £80,000.
  • The scenes set in Scotland were filmed on location at Urquhart Castle on the banks of Loch Ness; Kilmartin Hall on Loch Meiklie; Nairn railway station.
  • The cemetery scene with Stanley Holloway was filmed at the Church of St Mary, Winkfield in Berkshire.
