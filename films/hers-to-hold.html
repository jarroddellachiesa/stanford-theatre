Hers to Hold

1943 ★ 90 min
Starring: Deanna Durbin, Joseph Cotten, Charles Winninger
Director: Frank Ryan
Writer: Lewis R. Foster
Producer: Felix Jackson

Hers to Hold (aka Three Smart Girls Join Up) is a 1943 American romantic musical comedy film and is the third film in Three Smart Girls trilogy. In Hers to Hold, Deanna Durbin reprises her role as Penny Craig, who is the only sister remaining at home.

Trivia

  • The studio announced this project in April.
  • The pair were replaced when the producer felt that the film should be about the relationship of the three Craig sisters at home rather than the submitted screenplay about Penny's relationship with the other workers at the aircraft plant.
  • In April Kay Van Riper was signed to do the script.
