Dixiana

1930
Starring: Bebe Daniels, Everett Marshall, Wheeler & Woolsey
Director: Luther Reed
Writer: Anne Caldwell

Dixiana is a 1930 American pre-Code musical film directed by Luther Reed and produced and distributed by RKO Radio Pictures. The final twenty minutes of the picture were photographed in Technicolor. The film stars Bebe Daniels, Everett Marshall, Bert Wheeler, Robert Woolsey, Joseph Cawthorn, Jobyna Howland, Ralf Harolde, Bill "Bojangles" Robinson (in his film debut) and Dorothy Lee.
