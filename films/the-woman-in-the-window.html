The Woman in the Window

1944 ★ 99 min
Starring: Edward G. Robinson, Joan Bennett, Raymond Massey
Director: Fritz Lang
Writer: Nunnally Johnson
Producer: Nunnally Johnson

The Woman in the Window is a 1944 American film noir directed by Fritz Lang and starring Edward G. Robinson and Joan Bennett, with Raymond Massey, Edmund Breon, and Dan Duryea in supporting roles. It tells the story of a middle-aged psychology professor who kills in self-defense the lover of a young femme fatale he just met while his family is on vacation. The film is based on J. H. Wallis' 1942 novel Once Off Guard.

Trivia

  • Nominated for: Academy Award for Best Original Dramatic or Comedy Score.
  • The film was originally titled Once Off Guard.
  • In May 1944, William Goetz changed the title to The Woman in the Window.
  • By late September 1943, Edward G. Robinson had already been cast and Johnson and Lang wanted to borrow Ida Lupino for the leading female role.
