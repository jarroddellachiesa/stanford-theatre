Harvey

Harvey is a 1950 American fantasy comedy-drama film directed by Henry Koster, based on the 1944 play by Mary Chase. It stars James Stewart, Josephine Hull, Charles Drake, Cecil Kellaway, Jesse White, Victoria Horne, Wallace Ford, and Peggy Dow.

Trivia

  • Won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress.
  • Also nominated for: Academy Award for Best Actor.
