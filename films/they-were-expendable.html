They Were Expendable

1945 ★ 135 min
Starring: Robert Montgomery, Donna Reed, Jack Holt
Director: John Ford, Robert Montgomery
Writer: Frank Wead, Norman Corwin
Producer: John Ford

They Were Expendable is a 1945 American war film directed by John Ford, starring Robert Montgomery and John Wayne, and featuring Donna Reed.

Trivia

  • Nominated for: Academy Award for Best Sound; Academy Award for Best Special Effects.
  • Following the acquisition of the film rights to White's novel They Were Expendable, MGM asked Ford to direct a film based on the book; Ford repeatedly refused due to his conflicting service in the Navy Field Photographic Unit.
  • During production, Ford fell from scaffolding and broke his leg.
  • Montgomery did so well that within a few years, he made the transition from actor to directing films.

Filming Locations

  • The film, which received extensive support from the Navy Department, was shot in Key Biscayne, Florida, and the Florida Keys.
