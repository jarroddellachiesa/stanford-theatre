M

1931 ★ 117 min
Studio: Nero-Film
Starring: Gustaf Gründgens, Peter Lorre, Otto Wernicke
Director: Fritz Lang
Writer: Fritz Lang, Thea von Harbou, Paul Falkenberg
Producer: Seymour Nebenzal

M is a 1931 German mystery thriller film directed by Fritz Lang and starring Peter Lorre as Hans Beckert, a serial killer who targets children, in his third screen role. Both Lang's first sound film and an early example of a procedural drama, M centres on the efforts of both a city's police force and its criminal syndicates to apprehend a serial child-murderer.

Trivia

  • Lang placed an advertisement in a newspaper in 1930 stating that his next film would be Mörder unter uns (Murderer Among Us) and that it was about a child murderer.
  • Lang made the film for Nero-Film, rather than with UFA or his own production company.
  • Working titles for the film included Eine Stadt sucht einen Mörder (A City Searches for a Murderer) and Dein Mörder sieht Dich an (Your Murderer Looks at You).
