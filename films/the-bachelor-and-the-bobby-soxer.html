The Bachelor and the Bobby-Soxer

1947 ★ 97 min
Starring: Cary Grant, Myrna Loy, Shirley Temple
Director: Irving Reis
Writer: Sidney Sheldon
Producer: Dore Schary

The Bachelor and the Bobby-Soxer (released as Bachelor Knight in the United Kingdom) is a 1947 American screwball romantic comedy film directed by Irving Reis and written by Sidney Sheldon. The film stars Cary Grant, Myrna Loy and Shirley Temple in a story about a teenager’s crush on an older man. Upon release, The Bachelor and the Bobby-Soxer was received positively by audiences and critics.

Trivia

  • Won the Academy Award for Best Writing, Original Screenplay.
