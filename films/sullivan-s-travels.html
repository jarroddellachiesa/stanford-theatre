Sullivan's Travels

1941 ★ 90 min
Starring: Joel McCrea, Veronica Lake, William Demarest
Director: Preston Sturges
Writer: Preston Sturges
Producer: Paul Jones, Buddy DeSylva, Preston Sturges

Sullivan's Travels is a 1941 American comedy-drama film written and directed by Preston Sturges. A satire of the film industry, it follows a famous Hollywood comedy director (Joel McCrea) who, longing to make a socially relevant drama, sets out to live as a tramp to gain life experience for his forthcoming film. Along the way he unites with a poor aspiring actress (Veronica Lake) who accompanies him.

Trivia

  • Selected for the National Board of Review: Top Ten Films.
  • Paramount purchased Sturges's script for Sullivan's Travels for $6,000.
  • Sturges wanted the film to begin with the prologue: "This is the story of a man who wanted to wash an elephant.
  • The elephant darn near ruined him." Paramount contracted with the Schlesinger Corp., who made the Warner Bros.

Filming Locations

  • Location shooting took place in Canoga Park, San Marino, Castaic and at Lockheed Air Terminal.
