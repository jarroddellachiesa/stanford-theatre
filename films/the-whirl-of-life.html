The Whirl of Life

1915 ★ 60 min
Starring: Vernon Castle, Irene Castle, Ruth Gordon
Writer: Catherine Carr

The Whirl of Life is a 1915 silent film built around and starring dance couple Vernon and Irene Castle and stage actor Arthur Stanford, his only film. Directed by Oliver D. Bailey, the film was produced by the Cort Film Corporation and distributed on State Rights basis. Some garments worn by the cast were designed by Titanic survivor Lucile Duff-Gordon. The film is now in the public domain.
