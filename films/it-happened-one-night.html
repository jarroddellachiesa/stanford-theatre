It Happened One Night

1934 ★ 105 min
Starring: Clark Gable, Claudette Colbert, Walter Connolly
Director: Frank Capra
Writer: Robert Riskin
Producer: Frank Capra, Harry Cohn

It Happened One Night is a 1934 American screwball comedy film produced and directed by Frank Capra. Claudette Colbert stars as a pampered socialite who tries to get out from under her father's thumb and falls in love with a roguish reporter, played by Clark Gable. The screenplay by Robert Riskin is based on the August 1933 short story "Night Bus" by Samuel Hopkins Adams, which provided the shooting title.

Trivia

  • Won the Academy Award for Best Picture.
  • Won the Academy Award for Best Actor.
  • Won the Academy Award for Best Actress.
  • Won the Academy Award for Best Director.
  • Won the Academy Award for Best Writing, Adapted Screenplay.
  • Won the National Board of Review Award for Best Film.
  • Neither Gable nor Colbert was the first choice to play the lead roles.
  • Miriam Hopkins rejected the part of Ellie.
  • Robert Montgomery and Myrna Loy were then offered the roles, but both turned down the script.
