How to Marry a Millionaire

1953 ★ 95 min
Starring: Marilyn Monroe, Betty Grable, Lauren Bacall
Director: Jean Negulesco
Writer: Nunnally Johnson
Producer: Nunnally Johnson

How to Marry a Millionaire is a 1953 American romantic comedy film directed by Jean Negulesco and written and produced by Nunnally Johnson. The screenplay by Johnson was based on the plays The Greeks Had a Word for It (1930) by Zoe Akins and Loco (1946) by Dale Eunson and Katherine Albert.

Trivia

  • Nominated for: Academy Award for Best Costume Design, Color.
  • Nunnally Johnson, who adapted the screenplay from two different plays, produced the picture. 20th Century-Fox started production on The Robe before it began How to Marry a Millionaire.
  • The film's cinematography was by Joseph MacDonald.
