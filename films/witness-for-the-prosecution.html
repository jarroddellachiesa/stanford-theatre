Witness for the Prosecution is a 1957 American legal mystery melodrama film directed by Billy Wilder and starring Tyrone Power, Marlene Dietrich, and Charles Laughton, with Elsa Lanchester and John Williams. The film, which has elements of bleak black comedy and film noir, is a courtroom drama set in the Old Bailey in London and is based on the 1953 play of the same title by Agatha Christie.

Trivia

  • Won the Golden Globe Award for Best Supporting Actress – Motion Picture.
  • Also nominated for: Academy Award for Best Picture; Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress; Academy Award for Best Actor; Academy Award for Best Director; and 2 more.
  • Based on Witness for the Prosecution (1953 play written by Agatha Christie).
  • Producers Arthur Hornblow and Edward Small bought the rights to the play for $450,000.
  • The play was adjusted to emphasize the character of the defence barrister.
  • Billy Wilder was signed to direct in April 1956.
