It's Always Fair Weather

1955 ★ 102 min
Starring: Gene Kelly, Dan Dailey, Cyd Charisse
Director: Gene Kelly, Stanley Donen
Writer: Betty Comden, Adolph Green
Producer: Arthur Freed

It's Always Fair Weather is a 1955 American musical romantic comedy film directed by Gene Kelly and Stanley Donen. The film was scripted by Betty Comden and Adolph Green, who also wrote the show's lyrics, with music by André Previn. It stars Kelly, Dan Dailey, Cyd Charisse, Dolores Gray, and dancer/choreographer Michael Kidd in his first film acting role.

Trivia

  • Nominated for: Academy Award for Best Writing, Original Screenplay; Academy Award for Best Original Musical Score.
  • Screenwriters Betty Comden and Adolph Green conceived It's Always Fair Weather as a sequel to 1949's On the Town, intending to reunite Gene Kelly with his co-stars Frank Sinatra and Jules Munshin.
  • Comden stated, "While we were trying to think of another idea for Gene, by chance we told him this story.
  • Gene liked it." Kelly instead persuaded them to re-envision their idea into a musical film.

Filming Locations

  • Principal photography began on October 13, 1954, and wrapped on March 15, 1955.
