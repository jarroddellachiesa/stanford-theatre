Beauty and the Boss

1932 ★ 66 min
Starring: David Manners, Warren William, Frederick Kerr
Director: Roy Del Ruth
Writer: Joe Jackson (talent manager)

Beauty and the Boss is a 1932 American pre-Code romantic comedy film directed by Roy Del Ruth and starring Marian Marsh, David Manners and Warren William. It was based on the 1927 Hungarian play A templom egere by Ladislas Fodor about a secretary who eventually marries her boss. An English language adaptation of the play by Benn Levy, entitled A Church Mouse, opened in London in early May 1931.
