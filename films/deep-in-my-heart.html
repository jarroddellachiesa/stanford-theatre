Deep in My Heart

1954 ★ 132 min
Starring: José Ferrer, Merle Oberon, Helen Traubel
Director: Stanley Donen
Writer: Leonard Spigelgass
Producer: Roger Edens

Deep in My Heart is a 1954 American biographical musical film about the life of operetta composer Sigmund Romberg, who wrote the music for The Student Prince, The Desert Song, and The New Moon, among others. Leonard Spigelgass adapted the film from Elliott Arnold's 1949 biography of the same name. Roger Edens produced, Stanley Donen directed and Eugene Loring choreographed.
