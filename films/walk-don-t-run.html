Walk Don't Run

Starring: Cary Grant, Samantha Eggar, Jim Hutton
Director: Charles Walters
Writer: Robert W. Russell, Garson Kanin
Producer: Sol C. Siegel

Walk, Don't Run is a 1966 American romantic comedy film directed by Charles Walters (in his final film as a director), and starring Cary Grant (in his final film role), Samantha Eggar, and Jim Hutton. It follows a travelling industrialist visiting Tokyo, as he shares accommodation with a male athlete and a female apartment tenant during the 1964 Summer Olympics.

Trivia

  • Filming locations were at the Hotel Okura Tokyo, Embassy of the United Kingdom, Tokyo, and Yoyogi National Gymnasium.
  • Samantha Eggar had just made The Collector and this was her first Hollywood comedy.
  • After filming, Grant retired from acting to focus on raising his daughter Jennifer.
