Sherlock Jr. is a 1924 American silent comedy film directed by and starring Buster Keaton and written by Clyde Bruckman, Jean Havez, and Joseph A. Mitchell. It features Kathryn McGuire, Joe Keaton, and Ward Crane.

Watch Online: Internet Archive

Trivia

  • Originally titled The Misfit, production began in January 1924 in Los Angeles.
  • Keaton initially hired Roscoe "Fatty" Arbuckle as his co-director for the film.
  • Keaton had been discovered by Arbuckle, whose career was at a standstill after being accused of raping Virginia Rappe in 1921.
