The Life and Death of Colonel Blimp

1943 ★ 163 min
Studio: Powell and Pressburger

Director: Michael Powell, Emeric Pressburger
Writer: Michael Powell, Emeric Pressburger
Producer: Michael Powell, Emeric Pressburger

The Life and Death of Colonel Blimp is a 1943 British romantic-war film written, produced and directed by the British film-making team of Michael Powell and Emeric Pressburger. It stars Roger Livesey, Deborah Kerr and Anton Walbrook. The title derives from the satirical Colonel Blimp comic strip by David Low, but the story is original.

Trivia

  • Selected for the National Board of Review: Top Ten Films.
  • Bracken was uncomfortable with Churchill's request, and replied he had no power to do so.
  • After Ministry of Information and War Office officials had viewed a rough cut, objections were withdrawn in May 1943.
  • Frau von Kalteneck, a friend of Theo Kretschmar-Schuldorff, was played by Roger Livesey's wife Ursula Jeans.

Filming Locations

  • The film was shot in four months at Denham Film Studios and on location in and around London.
  • The (Wynne family home) was filmed at Denton Hall in Yorkshire.
