Jailhouse Rock

1957 ★ 96 min
Starring: Elvis Presley, Judy Tyler, Mickey Shaughnessy
Director: Richard Thorpe
Writer: Guy Trosper
Producer: Pandro S. Berman

Jailhouse Rock is a 1957 American musical drama film directed by Richard Thorpe and starring Elvis Presley. Adapted by Guy Trosper from a story written by Nedrick Young, the film tells the story of Vince Everett (Presley), a convict who learns the guitar while in prison and becomes a star following his release.

Trivia

  • The film was originally titled The Hard Way and was changed to Jailhouse Kid before MGM finally settled on Jailhouse Rock.
  • The film was not listed with the studio's planned releases for the year because it was based on a story by Nedrick Young, a blacklisted writer.
  • During production, producer Pandro Berman was more focused on another of his productions, the 1958 film The Brothers Karamazov.

Filming Locations

  • Jailhouse Rock was Presley's third film and his first for Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer studios, filmed in Culver City, California.
