High and Low

1963 ★ 143 min
Starring: Toshirō Mifune, Tatsuya Nakadai, Isao Kimura
Director: Akira Kurosawa
Writer: Hideo Oguni, Akira Kurosawa, Ryūzō Kikushima
Producer: Ryūzō Kikushima

High and Low (Japanese: 天国と地獄, Hepburn: Tengoku to Jigoku; lit. 'Heaven and Hell') is a 1963 Japanese police procedural film directed by Akira Kurosawa. It was written by Kurosawa, Hideo Oguni, Eijirō Hisaita, and Ryūzō Kikushima as a loose adaptation of the 1959 novel King's Ransom by Evan Hunter.

Trivia

  • Based on King's Ransom (1959 novel by Evan Hunter).
  • High and Low's screenplay was co-written by Akira Kurosawa, Hideo Oguni, Eijirō Hisaita, and Ryūzō Kikushima.
  • The story is based on Evan Hunter's novel King's Ransom (1959).
  • Toho purchased the rights to adapt the novel in 1961 for $5,000.

Filming Locations

  • Yokohama
  • TOHO Studios Co., Ltd.
  • High and Low was filmed at Toho Studios and on location in Yokohama.
  • Filming began on 2 September 1962 with the first act, the majority of which was filmed at Toho Studios.
  • The Yokohama exteriors were filmed in January 1963, but the cold weather made it difficult to act convincingly as if it were summer.
