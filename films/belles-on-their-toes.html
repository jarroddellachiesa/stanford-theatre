Belles on Their Toes is a 1952 American family comedy film directed by Henry Levin and starring Jeanne Crain, Myrna Loy, Debra Paget, Jeffrey Hunter and Edward Arnold. The screenplay was written by Henry Ephron and Phoebe Ephron based on the autobiographical book Belles on Their Toes (1950) by siblings Frank Bunker Gilbreth Jr. and Ernestine Gilbreth Carey.
