Murder He Says

1945 ★ 91 min
Starring: Fred MacMurray, Helen Walker, Marjorie Main
Director: George Marshall
Writer: Lou Breslow

Murder, He Says is a 1945 American black comedy horror film directed by George Marshall and starring Fred MacMurray, Helen Walker, and Marjorie Main. The plot follows a murderous rural family and the hapless pollster who becomes entangled in their hunt for a cache of money.
