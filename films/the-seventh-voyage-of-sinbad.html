The Seventh Voyage of Sinbad

Director: Nathan Juran

The 7th Voyage of Sinbad is a 1958 American fantasy adventure film directed by Nathan Juran and starring Kerwin Mathews, Kathryn Grant, Torin Thatcher, Richard Eyer and Alec Mango. It was distributed by Columbia Pictures and produced by Charles H. Schneer. It is the first of three Sinbad feature films produced by Schneer and conceptualized by Ray Harryhausen.

Trivia

  • Based on Sinbad the Sailor (fictional sailor).
  • Producer Charles H. Schneer announced the production in June 1957.
  • Schneer left for Europe to scout locations on 15 July.
  • Filming started the following month in Granada, Spain.

Filming Locations

  • Madrid
  • Mallorca
