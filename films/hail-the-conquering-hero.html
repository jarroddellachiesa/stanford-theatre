Hail the Conquering Hero is a 1944 American satirical comedy-drama film written and directed by Preston Sturges that stars Eddie Bracken, Ella Raines and William Demarest, supported by Raymond Walburn, Franklin Pangborn, Elizabeth Patterson, Bill Edwards and Freddie Steele.

Trivia

  • Selected for the National Board of Review: Top Ten Films.
  • Also nominated for: Academy Award for Best Writing, Original Screenplay.
  • Hail the Conquering Hero had a number of working titles on its way to the screen.
  • An early title was "Praise the Lord and Pass the Ammunition", and "Once Upon a Hero" and "The Little Marine" were also used.
  • I should have known about tilting at windmills. (Raines' career did not last long in any event: she retired in 1957.) It was customary at the time for the War Department to review scripts which dealt with military matters, but the revisions they requested were minor.
