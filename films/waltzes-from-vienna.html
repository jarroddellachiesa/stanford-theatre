Waltzes from Vienna

1934 ★ 80 min
Studio: Gaumont-British Picture Corporation
Starring: Esmond Knight, Jessie Matthews, Edmund Gwenn
Director: Alfred Hitchcock
Writer: Alma Reville, Guy Bolton
Producer: Tom Arnold

Waltzes from Vienna is a 1934 British biographical film directed by Alfred Hitchcock, sometimes known as Strauss' Great Waltz. It was part of the cycle of operetta films made in Britain during the 1930s. Hitchcock's film is based on the stage musical Waltzes from Vienna, which premiered in Vienna in October 1930.

Trivia

  • The film tells the story of the writing and performance of The Blue Danube.
  • According to Hitchcock: Waltzes from Vienna gave me many opportunities for working out ideas in the relation of film and music.
  • Naturally every cut in the film was worked out on the script before shooting began.
