It Started with Eve

1941 ★ 90 min
Starring: Deanna Durbin, Robert Cummings, Charles Laughton
Director: Henry Koster
Writer: Norman Krasna
Producer: Joe Pasternak

It Started with Eve is a 1941 American musical romantic comedy film starring Deanna Durbin, Charles Laughton, and Robert Cummings. Directed by Henry Koster, the film received an Oscar nomination for Best Original Music Score (Charles Previn and Hans J. Salter).

Trivia

  • Nominated for: Academy Award for Best Original Musical Score.
  • The film was originally known as Almost an Angel.
  • Joe Pasternak announced he would make the picture in 1938 as a vehicle for Danielle Darrieux.
  • Ralph Bock and Frederick Kohner wrote a script.
