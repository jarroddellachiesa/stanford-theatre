Top Hat

1935 ★ 101 min
Starring: Ginger Rogers, Fred Astaire, Erik Rhodes
Director: Mark Sandrich
Writer: Allan Scott, Dwight Taylor
Producer: Pandro S. Berman

Top Hat is a 1935 American musical comedy film, in which Fred Astaire plays an American tap dancer named Jerry Travers, who arrives in London to star in a show produced by Horace Hardwick (Edward Everett Horton). He meets and attempts to impress Dale Tremont (Ginger Rogers) to win her affection.

Trivia

  • Nominated for: Academy Award for Best Picture; Academy Award for Best Production Design.
  • Top Hat began filming on April 1, 1935, and cost $620,000 to make.
  • Shooting ended in June and the first public previews were held in July.
  • A further four minutes were cut before its premiere at the Radio City Music Hall, where it broke all records, went on to gross $3 million on its initial release, and became RKO's most profitable film of the 1930s.
