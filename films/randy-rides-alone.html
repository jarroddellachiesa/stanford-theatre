Randy Rides Alone

1934 ★ 53 min
Studio: Monogram Pictures, Lone Star Productions
Starring: John Wayne, George "Gabby" Hayes, Yakima Canutt
Director: Harry L. Fraser
Writer: Lindsley Parsons
Producer: Paul Malvern

Randy Rides Alone is a 1934 American Pre-Code Western film starring John Wayne, Yakima Canutt, and George Hayes (before Hayes developed his famous "Gabby" persona). The 53-minute black-and-white film was directed by Harry L. Fraser, produced by Paul Malvern for Lone Star Productions, and released by Monogram Pictures.

Watch Online: Internet Archive

Trivia

  • The Lone Star pictures began at Warner Bros. as a way of repurposing stock footage from Ken Maynard's films with Wayne acting in the closeups.
  • Trem Carr suggested continuing the brand with original films.
  • Wayne agreed to do eight pictures a year at a rate of $2,500.

Filming Locations

  • Santa Clarita
  • The film was shot from late April to early May near Placerita Canyon and in Santa Clarita, California.
