Imitation of Life is a 1959 American melodrama film directed by Douglas Sirk and produced by Ross Hunter. It stars Lana Turner and John Gavin, with Sandra Dee, Dan O'Herlihy, Susan Kohner, Robert Alda, and Juanita Moore in supporting roles. Gospel music singer Mahalia Jackson appears as a church choir soloist.

Trivia

  • Nominated for: Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress.
  • Based on Imitation of Life (1933 novel by Fannie Hurst).
  • The screenplay was written by Eleanore Griffin and Allan Scott.
  • With director Douglas Sirk, they made changes to the story to reflect the society of 1959.
  • In the novel, the Lora character, Bea Pullman, became successful by commercial production of her maid Delilah's family waffle recipe, a pancake recipe in the 1934 film version.
