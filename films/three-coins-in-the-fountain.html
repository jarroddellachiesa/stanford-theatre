Three Coins in the Fountain is a 1954 American romantic comedy drama film directed by Jean Negulesco from a screenplay by John Patrick, based on the 1952 novel Coins in the Fountain by John H. Secondari. It stars Clifton Webb, Dorothy McGuire, Jean Peters, Louis Jourdan, and Maggie McNamara, with Rossano Brazzi, Howard St. John, Kathryn Givney, and Cathleen Nesbitt.

Trivia

  • Won the Academy Award for Best Cinematography, Color.
  • Also nominated for: Academy Award for Best Picture.
  • Based on Coins in the Fountain (1952 novel by John H. Secondari).
