Santa Fe Trail

1940 ★ 110 min
Starring: Errol Flynn, Olivia de Havilland, Raymond Massey
Director: Michael Curtiz
Writer: Robert Buckner
Producer: Hal B. Wallis

Santa Fe Trail is a 1940 American historical drama film directed by Michael Curtiz and starring Errol Flynn (as J. E. B. "Jeb" Stuart), Olivia de Havilland, Raymond Massey (as John Brown), Ronald Reagan (as George Armstrong Custer) and Alan Hale. Written by Robert Buckner, the film focuses largely on the abolitionist John Brown and his controversial campaign against slavery before the American Civil War.

Trivia

  • Raymond Massey signed to play John Brown in June.
