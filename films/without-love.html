Without Love

1945 ★ 111 min
Starring: Spencer Tracy, Katharine Hepburn, Elliott Nugent
Director: Harold S. Bucquet
Writer: Donald Ogden Stewart, Frances Marion
Producer: Lawrence Weingarten

Without Love is a 1945 romantic comedy film directed by Harold S. Bucquet and starring Spencer Tracy, Katharine Hepburn, and Lucille Ball. Based on a 1942 play by Philip Barry, the film's screenplay was written by Donald Ogden Stewart.

Trivia

  • The original Philip Barry stage play debuted on Broadway at the St. James Theatre in 1942.
  • Katharine Hepburn starred as Jamie Rowan with actor/writer/director Elliott Nugent as Patrick Jamieson, the role Spencer Tracy would take in the film.
  • Audrey Christie played the Lucille Ball role of Kitty Trimble, and the cast included Royal Beal and Lauren Gilbert.
