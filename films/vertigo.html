Vertigo

1958 ★ 128 min
Starring: James Stewart, Kim Novak, Barbara Bel Geddes
Director: Alfred Hitchcock
Writer: Alec Coppel, Samuel A. Taylor, Maxwell Anderson
Producer: Alfred Hitchcock

Vertigo is a 1958 American psychological thriller film produced and directed by Alfred Hitchcock. The story was based on the 1954 novel D'entre les morts (From Among the Dead) by Boileau-Narcejac, with a screenplay by Alec Coppel and Samuel A. Taylor.

Watch Online: YouTube

Trivia

  • Nominated for: Academy Award for Best Production Design; Academy Award for Best Sound.
  • Based on The Living and the Dead (1954 novel by Boileau-Narcejac).
  • The screenplay of Vertigo is an adaptation of the 1954 French novel D'entre les morts (From Among the Dead) by Pierre Boileau and Thomas Narcejac.
  • Hitchcock had attempted to buy the rights to the previous novel by the same authors, Celle qui n'était plus (She Who Was No More), but failed, and it was instead adapted by Henri-Georges Clouzot as Les Diaboliques.
  • In the book, Judy's involvement in Madeleine's death was not revealed until the denouement; at the scriptwriting stage, Hitchcock suggested revealing the secret two-thirds of the way through the film so that the audience would understand Judy's dilemma.
