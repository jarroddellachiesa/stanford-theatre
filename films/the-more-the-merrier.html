The More the Merrier

1943 ★ 104 min
Starring: Jean Arthur, Joel McCrea, Charles Coburn
Director: George Stevens
Writer: Lewis R. Foster, Frank Ross, Richard Flournoy
Producer: George Stevens

The More the Merrier is a 1943 American romantic comedy film produced and directed by George Stevens, and starring Jean Arthur, Joel McCrea, and Charles Coburn. The film's script—from Two's a Crowd, an original screenplay by Garson Kanin (uncredited)—was written by Robert Russell, Frank Ross, Richard Flournoy, and Lewis R. Foster.

Trivia

  • Won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor.
  • Also nominated for: Academy Award for Best Picture; Academy Award for Best Actress; Academy Award for Best Director; Academy Award for Best Story; and 1 more.
  • Jean Arthur got the ball rolling on The More The Merrier, paying playwright and writer Garson Kanin $25,000 to adapt his short story "Two's a Crowd" into a screenplay.
  • Kanin co-wrote the script with Robert Russell and Frank Ross, Arthur's husband.
  • Arthur also brought director George Stevens (with whom she had recently worked on 1942's The Talk of the Town) and co-star Joel McCrea to the project.
