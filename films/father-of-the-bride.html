Father of the Bride

1950 ★ 92 min

1950 film by Vincente Minnelli

Father of the Bride is a 1950 American romantic comedy film directed by Vincente Minnelli from a screenplay by Frances Goodrich and Albert Hackett, based on the 1949 eponymous novel by Edward Streeter. The film stars Spencer Tracy, Joan Bennett, and Elizabeth Taylor, and follows a man trying to cope with preparations for his daughter's wedding.

Trivia

  • Nominated for: Academy Award for Best Picture; Academy Award for Best Actor; Academy Award for Best Writing, Adapted Screenplay.
  • Based on Father of the Bride (novel by Edward Streeter).
  • According to Frank Miller for TCM, when creating the role for the father, the character was shaped around Spencer Tracy.
  • Minnelli believed Tracy was capable of handling a role that balanced humor with fatherly tenderness.
  • After some miscommunication with the producers, Jack Benny was brought in for a screen test.
