The Solid Gold Cadillac

1956 ★ 99 min
Starring: Judy Holliday, Paul Douglas, Fred Clark
Director: Richard Quine
Writer: George S. Kaufman
Producer: Fred Kohlmar

The Solid Gold Cadillac is a 1956 American comedy film directed by Richard Quine and written by Abe Burrows, Howard Teichmann, and George S. Kaufman. It was adapted from the hit Broadway play of the same name by Teichmann and Kaufman that pillories big business and corrupt businessmen. The film stars Judy Holliday and Paul Douglas.

Trivia

  • Won the Academy Award for Best Costume Design, Black-and-White.
  • Also nominated for: Academy Award for Best Art Direction, Black and White.
