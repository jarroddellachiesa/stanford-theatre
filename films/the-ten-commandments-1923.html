The Ten Commandments

1923 ★ 146 min
Starring: Theodore Roberts, Charles de Rochefort, Richard Dix
Director: Cecil B. DeMille
Writer: Jeanie MacPherson
Producer: Cecil B. DeMille

The Ten Commandments is a 1923 American silent religious epic film produced and directed by Cecil B. DeMille. Written by Jeanie MacPherson, the film is divided into two parts: a prologue recreating the biblical story of the Exodus and a modern story concerning two brothers and their respective views of the Ten Commandments.

Trivia

  • In 1922, while he was still filming Adam's Rib, Cecil B. DeMille decided to let the public choose the subject of his next production.
  • Because this was the first time a filmmaker asked the public for ideas, other newspapers published stories about the contest and thousands of letters eventually came in from many parts of the world.
  • In his autobiography, DeMille remembered, "Here was an idea, bigger than any I had attempted to put on the screen.
