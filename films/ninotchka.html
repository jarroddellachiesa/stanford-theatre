Ninotchka

1939 ★ 110 min
Starring: Greta Garbo, Bela Lugosi, Melvyn Douglas
Director: Ernst Lubitsch
Writer: Billy Wilder, Walter Reisch, Charles Brackett
Producer: Ernst Lubitsch, Sidney Franklin

Ninotchka is a 1939 American romantic comedy film made by Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer, produced and directed by Ernst Lubitsch and stars Greta Garbo and Melvyn Douglas. The film was written by Billy Wilder, Charles Brackett and Walter Reisch, based on a story by Melchior Lengyel.

Trivia

  • Selected for the National Board of Review: Top Ten Films.
  • Also nominated for: Academy Award for Best Actress; Academy Award for Best Story; Academy Award for Best Writing, Adapted Screenplay; Academy Award for Best Picture.
