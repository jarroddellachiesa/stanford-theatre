Full House

1952 ★ 118 min
Starring: Michel Simon, Reinhard Kolldehoff, Inge Landgut
Director: Henri Verneuil
Writer: Jacques Companéez, André Tabet
Producer: Raymond Froment

Full House (French: Brelan d'as, lit. 'Three Aces') is a 1952 French crime mystery anthology film directed by Henri Verneuil and starring Michel Simon, Raymond Rouleau, John Van Dreelen, Arlette Merry and Nathalie Nattier. It consists of three segments, each featuring a popular detective character: Jules Maigret, Monsieur Wens and Lemmy Caution.
