Lawrence of Arabia

1962 ★ 216 min
Studio: Horizon Pictures
Starring: Jack Hawkins, Anthony Quinn, Peter O'Toole
Director: David Lean
Writer: Robert Bolt, Michael Wilson, T. E. Lawrence
Producer: Sam Spiegel

Lawrence of Arabia is a 1962 epic biographical drama film directed by David Lean and produced by Sam Spiegel through the latter's British company Horizon Pictures and distributed by Columbia Pictures. It is based on the life of T. E. Lawrence and his 1926 book Seven Pillars of Wisdom (also known as Revolt in the Desert). Lawrence of Arabia introduces Peter O'Toole as Lawrence, with Alec Guinness playing Prince Faisal.

Trivia

  • Won the Academy Award for Best Picture.
  • Won the Silver nugget for the best foreign film.
  • Won the Academy Award for Best Director.
  • Won the Academy Award for Best Cinematography, Color.
  • Won the Academy Award for Best Film Editing.
  • Won the Academy Award for Best Art Direction, Color.
  • Won the Academy Award for Best Original Score.
  • Won the Academy Award for Best Sound.
  • Selected for the National Board of Review: Top Ten Films.
  • Also nominated for: Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor; Academy Award for Best Actor; Academy Award for Best Writing, Adapted Screenplay.
  • Previous films about T. E. Lawrence had been planned but had not been made.
  • In the 1940s, Alexander Korda was interested in filming Seven Pillars of Wisdom with Laurence Olivier, Leslie Howard, or Robert Donat as Lawrence, but had to pull out owing to financial difficulties.
  • David Lean had been approached to direct a 1952 version for the Rank Organisation, but the project fell through.

Filming Locations

  • Spain
  • London
  • Morocco
  • Jordan
  • Almería Province
  • The desert scenes were shot in Jordan and Morocco and Almería and Doñana in Spain.
  • The execution of Gasim, the train attacks, and Deraa exteriors were filmed in the Almería region, with some of the filming being delayed because of a flash flood.
  • All of the interiors were shot in Spain, including Lawrence's first meeting with Faisal and the scene in Auda's tent.
