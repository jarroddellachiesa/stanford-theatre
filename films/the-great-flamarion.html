The Great Flamarion

1945 ★ 78 min
Starring: Erich von Stroheim, Mary Beth Hughes, Dan Duryea
Director: Anthony Mann
Writer: Heinz Herald
Producer: W. Lee Wilder

The Great Flamarion is a 1945 American film noir mystery film directed by Anthony Mann starring Erich von Stroheim and Mary Beth Hughes. The film, like many films noirs, is shot in flashback narrative. The film was produced by Republic Pictures. This film is now in the public domain.
