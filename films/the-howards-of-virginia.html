The Howards of Virginia

1940 ★ 116 min
Starring: Cary Grant, Martha Scott, Cedric Hardwicke
Director: Frank Lloyd

The Howards of Virginia is a 1940 American drama war Western film directed by Frank Lloyd, released by Columbia Pictures, and based on the book The Tree of Liberty written by Elizabeth Page.
