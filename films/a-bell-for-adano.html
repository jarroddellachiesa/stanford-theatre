A Bell for Adano

1945 ★ 103 min
Starring: Gene Tierney, John Hodiak, William Bendix
Director: Henry King
Writer: Norman Reilly Raine, Lamar Trotti, John Hersey
Producer: Louis D. Lighton, Lamar Trotti

A Bell for Adano is a 1945 American war film directed by Henry King and starring John Hodiak and Gene Tierney. It was adapted from the 1944 novel of the same title by John Hersey, which won the Pulitzer Prize for fiction in 1945. The story had been staged as a Broadway play in 1944 starring Fredric March.

Trivia

  • The character of Joppolo is based on the real-life experiences of Frank Toscani, who was the military governor of the town of Licata, Sicily after the Allied invasion.
