Random Harvest

1942 ★ 125 min
Starring: Ronald Colman, Greer Garson, Philip Dorn
Director: Mervyn LeRoy
Writer: Claudine West, George Froeschel, Arthur Wimperis
Producer: Sidney Franklin

Random Harvest is a 1942 American romantic drama film based on the 1941 James Hilton novel of the same title, directed by Mervyn LeRoy. Claudine West, George Froeschel, and Arthur Wimperis adapted the novel for the screen, and received an Academy Award nomination.

Trivia

  • Nominated for: Academy Award for Best Picture; Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress; Academy Award for Best Actor; Academy Award for Best Director; and 3 more.
  • Based on Random Harvest (1941 novel by James Hilton).
