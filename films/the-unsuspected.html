The Unsuspected

1947 ★ 103 min
Starring: Joan Caulfield, Claude Rains, Audrey Totter
Director: Michael Curtiz
Writer: Ranald MacDougall
Producer: Michael Curtiz, Charles Hoffman

The Unsuspected is a 1947 American mystery film noir directed by Michael Curtiz and starring Claude Rains, Joan Caulfield, Audrey Totter, Constance Bennett, Hurd Hatfield, and Michael North. The film was based on the 1946 novel of the same title by Charlotte Armstrong. The screenplay was co-written by Bess Meredyth, who was married to director Curtiz.

Trivia

  • The Unsuspected was originally a serial in The Saturday Evening Post, running from August 11 to September 29, 1945.
  • Pictures purchased the film rights prior to publication, and in May 1946, they announced that Michael Curtiz would direct.
  • Ranald MacDougall wrote the first screenplay.
