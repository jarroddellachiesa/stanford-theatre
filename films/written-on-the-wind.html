Written on the Wind

1956 ★ 99 min
Starring: Rock Hudson, Lauren Bacall, Robert Stack
Director: Douglas Sirk
Writer: Robert Wilder
Producer: Albert Zugsmith

Written on the Wind is a 1956 American Southern Gothic melodrama film directed by Douglas Sirk and starring Rock Hudson, Lauren Bacall, Robert Stack, and Dorothy Malone. It follows the complicated relationships among dysfunctional family members of a Texas oil dynasty: its alcoholic heir, his wife (a former secretary for the family company), his childhood best friend, and his ruthless, self-destructive sister.

Trivia

  • Won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress.
  • Also nominated for: Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor.
  • Based on Written on the Wind (1946 novel).
  • The film's source novel by Robert Wilder was inspired by the life and death of Zachary Smith Reynolds, son of R. J. Reynolds and heir to the Reynolds Tobacco fortune, who died from a gunshot wound to the head at his family's estate after a birthday party.
  • The novel had been optioned for a feature film adaptation by RKO Pictures in 1945 before the rights were sold to International Pictures the following year after RKO shelved the project.
  • In 1946, Universal Pictures acquired the rights to the novel after absorbing International Pictures; however, the project remained in limbo due to pressure from the Reynolds family, who threatened to launch a lawsuit against any film version of Wilder's novel.

Filming Locations

  • Principal photography began on November 28, 1955, in Los Angeles on the Universal Studios lot.
