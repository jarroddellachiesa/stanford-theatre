Ruby Gentry

1952 ★ 82 min
Starring: Jennifer Jones, Charlton Heston, Karl Malden
Director: King Vidor

Ruby Gentry is a 1952 American southern Gothic film noir directed by King Vidor, and starring Jennifer Jones, Charlton Heston, and Karl Malden. Set in the backwoods of North Carolina, the film follows a poor woman who marries a man for his wealth, leading to tragic events. Twentieth Century-Fox premiered Ruby Gentry in Salt Lake City on December 19, 1952, with releases expanding to numerous cities through 1953.

Trivia

  • King Vidor, who was hired as director, had previously directed actress Jennifer Jones in Duel in the Sun (1946), produced by Jones's husband, David O. Selznick.
  • Vidor had directed Bird of Paradise (1932), also produced by Selznick, decades earlier.
  • During the filming of Duel in the Sun, tensions rose between Vidor and Selznick near the production's completion due to what Vidor felt was "near-constant intervention" from Selznick.

Filming Locations

  • Though some establishing shots were completed in North Carolina, Ruby Gentry was mostly filmed in California in Paso Robles, San Luis Obispo, San Marino, Morro Bay, Pismo Beach, and director King Vidor's private ranch in Willow Creek.
