The Bridge on the River Kwai

1957 ★ 156 min
Studio: Horizon Pictures
Starring: Jack Hawkins, William Holden, Sessue Hayakawa
Director: David Lean
Writer: Carl Foreman, Michael Wilson, Calder Willingham
Producer: Sam Spiegel

The Bridge on the River Kwai is a 1957 epic war film directed by David Lean and based on the novel The Bridge over the River Kwai, written by Pierre Boulle. Boulle's novel and the film's screenplay are fictional; they use the construction of the Burma Railway in 1942–1943 as historical setting.
