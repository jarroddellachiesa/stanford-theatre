Parash Pathar (The Philosopher's Stone)

1958 ★ 90 min
Starring: Kali Banerjee, Chhabi Biswas, Pahari Sanyal
Director: Satyajit Ray
Writer: Satyajit Ray

Parash Pathar (Bengali: পরশ পাথর Porosh Pathor; English: The Philosopher's Stone; French: La Pierre Philosophale) is a 1958 Indian Bengali language fantasy comedy film. It was Satyajit Ray's first film outside of The Apu Trilogy. It was also his first comedy and first magical realist film.
