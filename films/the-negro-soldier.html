The Negro Soldier

1944 ★ 43 min
Starring: Carlton Moss
Director: Stuart Heisler
Writer: Carlton Moss
Producer: Frank Capra, United States Department of War

The Negro Soldier is a 1944 documentary film created by the United States Army during World War II. It was produced by Frank Capra as a follow-up to his successful film series Why We Fight. The army used the film as propaganda to convince black Americans to enlist in the army and fight in the war.
