Alexander's Ragtime Band

1938 ★ 108 min
Starring: Tyrone Power, Alice Faye, Don Ameche
Director: Henry King
Writer: Lamar Trotti, Darryl F. Zanuck, Irving Berlin
Producer: Darryl F. Zanuck

Alexander's Ragtime Band is a 1938 American musical film released by 20th Century Fox that takes its name from the 1911 Irving Berlin song "Alexander's Ragtime Band" to tell a story of a society boy who scandalizes his family by pursuing a career in ragtime instead of "serious" music.

Trivia

  • Won the Academy Award for Best Score, Adaptation or Treatment.
  • Also nominated for: Academy Award for Best Picture; Academy Award for Best Story; Academy Award for Best Film Editing; Academy Award for Best Production Design.
