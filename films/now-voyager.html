Now, Voyager

1942 ★ 117 min
Starring: Bette Davis, Paul Henreid, Claude Rains
Director: Irving Rapper
Writer: Casey Robinson
Producer: Hal B. Wallis

Now, Voyager is a 1942 American drama film starring Bette Davis, Paul Henreid, and Claude Rains, and directed by Irving Rapper. The screenplay by Casey Robinson is based on the 1941 novel of the same name by Olive Higgins Prouty.

Trivia

  • Won the Academy Award for Best Original Dramatic or Comedy Score.
  • Also nominated for: Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress; Academy Award for Best Actress.
  • Filming ran from April 7 to June 23 of 1942 as producer Hal B. Wallis made Now, Voyager his first independent production at Warner Bros. under a new arrangement with the studio.
  • The initial choices for Charlotte were Irene Dunne, Norma Shearer, and Ginger Rogers.
  • More than any other of her previous films, Davis became absorbed in the role, not only reading the original novel, but also becoming involved in details such as choosing her wardrobe personally.
