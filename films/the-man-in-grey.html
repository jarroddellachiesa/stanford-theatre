The Man in Grey

1943 ★ 116 min
Studio: Gainsborough Pictures
Starring: Margaret Lockwood, James Mason, Phyllis Calvert
Director: Leslie Arliss
Writer: Margaret Kennedy, Doreen Montgomery
Producer: Edward Black

The Man in Grey is a 1943 British melodrama film made by Gainsborough Pictures; it is considered to be the first of a series of period costume dramas now known as the "Gainsborough melodramas". It was directed by Leslie Arliss and produced by Edward Black from a screenplay by Arliss and Margaret Kennedy that was adapted by Doreen Montgomery from the 1941 novel The Man in Grey by Eleanor Smith.

Trivia

  • Based on The Man in Grey (novel by Eleanor Smith).
  • Margaret Lockwood later wrote that when she heard about the project, she read the novel and thought she would be ideal for the role of Clarissa.
  • Lockwood says that James Mason's role was originally offered to Eric Portman who turned it down.
  • To play the role, Mason agreed to make an extra five pictures for Gainsborough (he wound up making only three due to the studio's failure to exercise an option in time for the other two).
