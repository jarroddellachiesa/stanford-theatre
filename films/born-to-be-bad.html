Born to Be Bad

1934 ★ 62 min
Starring: Loretta Young, Cary Grant, Jackie Kelk
Director: Lowell Sherman
Writer: Ralph Graves
Producer: Darryl F. Zanuck

Born to Be Bad is a 1934 American pre-Code drama film starring Loretta Young and Cary Grant, and directed by Lowell Sherman. This film was rejected by the Hays Office twice before it was finally approved. Young's character, an unwed mother who entertains wholesale buyers to secure contracts for her friend, had to be re-written and re-filmed, so that her occupation is only hinted at.
