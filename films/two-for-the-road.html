Two for the Road

1967 ★ 111 min
Starring: Albert Finney, Audrey Hepburn, William Daniels
Director: Stanley Donen
Writer: Frederic Raphael
Producer: Stanley Donen

Two for the Road is a 1967 romantic comedy-drama directed and produced by Stanley Donen, starring Audrey Hepburn and Albert Finney. The film tells the story of a married couple who reflect on their twelve-year relationship while on a road trip from England to the French Riviera.

Trivia

  • Nominated for: Academy Award for Best Writing, Original Screenplay.
  • Raphael conceived the idea for Two for the Road in 1963 while driving with his wife from London to Rome.
  • Ten years seemed a long time in those days.
  • Janni thought it was a good idea, but did not pursue it.

Filming Locations

  • Paris
  • Saint-Tropez
  • Nice
  • The Dalbret scenes were filmed at a villa in L'Oumède, Ramatuelle.
