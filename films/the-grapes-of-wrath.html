The Grapes of Wrath

1940 ★ 128 min
Starring: Henry Fonda, Jane Darwell, John Carradine
Director: John Ford
Writer: Nunnally Johnson, John Steinbeck
Producer: Darryl F. Zanuck, Nunnally Johnson

The Grapes of Wrath is a 1940 American drama film directed by John Ford. It was based on John Steinbeck's 1939 Pulitzer Prize-winning novel of the same name. The screenplay was written by Nunnally Johnson and the executive producer was Darryl F. Zanuck.

Trivia

  • Won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress.
  • Won the Academy Award for Best Director.
  • Selected for the National Board of Review: Top Ten Films.
  • Won the National Board of Review Award for Best Film.
  • Also nominated for: Academy Award for Best Actor; Academy Award for Best Film Editing; Academy Award for Best Writing, Adapted Screenplay; Academy Award for Best Picture; and 1 more.
  • Based on The Grapes of Wrath (1939 novel by John Steinbeck).
  • Executive producer Darryl F. Zanuck was nervous about the left-wing political views of the novel, especially the ending.
  • Due to the red-baiting common to the era, Darryl Zanuck sent private investigators to Oklahoma to help him legitimize the film.
  • Critic Roger Ebert believed that World War II also helped sell the film's message, as Communism received a brief respite from American demonizing during that period.
