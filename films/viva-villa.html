Viva Villa!

1934 ★ 109 min
Starring: Fay Wray, Wallace Beery, Leo Carrillo
Director: Howard Hawks, Jack Conway, John Waters
Writer: Howard Hawks, Ben Hecht, James Kevin McGuinness
Producer: David O. Selznick

Viva Villa! is a 1934 American pre-Code Western film directed by Jack Conway and starring Wallace Beery as Mexican revolutionary Pancho Villa. The screenplay was written by Ben Hecht, adapted from the 1933 book Viva Villa! by Edgecumb Pinchon and O. B. Stade. Parts of the film were shot on location in Mexico and produced by David O. Selznick.

Trivia

  • Selected for the National Board of Review: Top Ten Films.
  • Also nominated for: Academy Award for Best Picture; Academy Award for Best Writing, Adapted Screenplay; Academy Award for Best Sound.
  • David O. Selznick began filming Viva Villa! in 1932 in Mexico.
  • Between filming and its release in April 1934, the film went through a development hell.
  • Initially, Lee Tracy was cast to play a role of Jonny Sykes.
