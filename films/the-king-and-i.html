The King and I

1956 ★ 133 min
Starring: Deborah Kerr, Yul Brynner, Rita Moreno
Director: Walter Lang
Writer: Ernest Lehman, Oscar Hammerstein II
Producer: Charles Brackett, Darryl F. Zanuck

The King and I is a 1956 American musical film made by 20th Century-Fox, directed by Walter Lang and produced by Charles Brackett and Darryl F. Zanuck. The screenplay by Ernest Lehman is based on the 1951 Rodgers and Hammerstein musical The King and I, which is itself based on the 1944 novel Anna and the King of Siam by Margaret Landon.

Trivia

  • Won the Academy Award for Best Actor.
  • Won the Academy Award for Best Costume Design, Color.
  • Won the Academy Award for Best Art Direction, Color.
  • Won the Academy Award for Best Original Musical Score.
  • Won the Academy Award for Best Sound.
  • Selected for the National Board of Review: Top Ten Films.
  • Also nominated for: Academy Award for Best Picture; Academy Award for Best Actress; Academy Award for Best Director; Academy Award for Best Cinematography, Color; and 1 more.
  • Based on The King and I (1951 musical by the team of composer Richard Rodgers and dramatist Oscar Hammerstein II).
  • Based on Anna and the King of Siam (1944 semi-fictionalized biographical novel by Margaret Landon).
  • The musical was written for Gertrude Lawrence, and her appearance in the film was contractually guaranteed.
  • Dinah Shore was considered for the role of Anna in the movie.
  • Maureen O'Hara was originally cast, but Richard Rodgers did not agree to the casting.
