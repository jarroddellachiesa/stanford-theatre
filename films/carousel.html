Carousel

1956 ★ 111 min
Starring: Gordon MacRae, Shirley Jones, Cameron Mitchell
Director: Henry King
Writer: Phoebe Ephron, Henry Ephron
Producer: Henry Ephron

Carousel is a 1956 American dramatic fantasy musical film based on the 1945 Rodgers and Hammerstein stage musical of the same name, which was based on Ferenc Molnár's 1909 non-musical play Liliom. The film stars Gordon MacRae and Shirley Jones and was directed by Henry King.

Trivia

  • Based on Liliom (1909 play by the Hungarian playwright Ferenc Molnár).
  • Based on Carousel (1945 musical by Richard Rodgers (music) and Oscar Hammerstein II (book and lyrics)).
  • All of the other prints were composite prints and used the standard four-track stereo soundtrack featured on regular CinemaScope films of the era.
  • Cinematographer Charles G. Clarke explained: "In the beginning it was decided to film 'Carousel' in both 55mm and the standard 35mm CinemaScope.
  • Gordon MacRae, Sinatra's replacement, was reported to have prepared in advance for the role, suspecting that Sinatra might withdraw.

  • The film was shot in CinemaScope 55 and DeLuxe Color.
