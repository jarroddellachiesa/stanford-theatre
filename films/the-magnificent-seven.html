The Magnificent Seven

1960 ★ 128 min
Studio: United Artists, The Mirisch Company
Starring: Yul Brynner, Eli Wallach, Steve McQueen
Director: John Sturges
Writer: Walter Newman, Walter Bernstein, William Roberts
Producer: John Sturges

The Magnificent Seven is a 1960 American action Western film directed by John Sturges. The screenplay, credited to William Roberts, is a remake – in an Old West-style – of Akira Kurosawa's 1954 Japanese film Seven Samurai (itself initially released in the United States as The Magnificent Seven).

Trivia

  • Nominated for: Academy Award for Best Original Dramatic or Comedy Score.
  • Based on Seven Samurai (1954 Japanese film by Akira Kurosawa).
  • Lou Morheim acquired rights to remake Seven Samurai in the US for $2,500.
  • Anthony Quinn was lined up to star with Brynner as director but later Martin Ritt was appointed as director with Brynner starring.
  • Brynner approached producer Walter Mirisch with the idea of remaking Kurosawa's famous samurai film.

Filming Locations

  • Mexico
  • Location shooting began on March 1, 1960, in Mexico, where both the village and the U.S. border town were built for the film.
