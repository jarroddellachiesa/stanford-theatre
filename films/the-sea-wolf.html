The Sea Wolf

1941 ★ 100 min
Starring: Edward G. Robinson, Ida Lupino, John Garfield
Director: Michael Curtiz
Writer: Robert Rossen, Jack London
Producer: Hal B. Wallis, Jack Warner

The Sea Wolf is a 1941 American adventure drama film adaptation of Jack London's 1904 novel The Sea-Wolf with Edward G. Robinson, Ida Lupino, John Garfield, and Alexander Knox making his debut in an American film. The film was written by Robert Rossen and directed by Michael Curtiz.

Trivia

  • Nominated for: Academy Award for Best Special Effects.
  • Based on The Sea-Wolf (novel by Jack London).
  • Robert Rossen's re-draft of the script may be the greatest influence on the film.
  • Rossen also split the novel's idealistic hero into an intellectual dishwasher and a rebellious seaman and gave the seaman a love interest, played by Lupino.
  • Rossen added scenes for this pair, partly urged by Lupino.
