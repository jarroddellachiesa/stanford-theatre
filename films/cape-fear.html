Cape Fear

1962 ★ 106 min
Starring: Gregory Peck, Robert Mitchum, Martin Balsam
Director: J. Lee Thompson
Writer: James R. Webb, John D. MacDonald
Producer: Sy Bartlett

Cape Fear is a 1962 American southern gothic psychological thriller film directed by J. Lee Thompson, from a screenplay by James R. Webb, adapting the 1957 novel The Executioners by John D. MacDonald.

Trivia

  • Based on The Executioners (1957 novel by John D. MacDonald).
  • Cornel Wilde acquired the rights to John D. MacDonald's novel The Executioners for $30,000 in 1958.
  • Gregory Peck had his own production company, Melville Productions, in partnership with Sy Bartlett, which had made The Big Country and Pork Chop Hill and they later purchased the rights.
  • Peck was impressed by J. Lee Thompson's work on that film and hired him for Cape Fear.

Filming Locations

  • California
  • Principal photography of Cape Fear began on April 6 and ended in June 1961.
  • The outdoor scenes were filmed on location in Savannah, Georgia; Stockton, California; and the Universal Studios backlot at Universal City, California.
  • The indoor scenes were done at Universal Studios Soundstage.
