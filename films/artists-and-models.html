Artists and Models

1937 ★ 95 min
Starring: Jack Benny, Ida Lupino, Gail Patrick
Director: Raoul Walsh
Writer: Walter DeLeon
Producer: Adolph Zukor

Artists and Models is a 1937 black-and-white American musical comedy film, directed by Raoul Walsh and starring Jack Benny and Ida Lupino. It was produced by Lewis E. Gensler. In 1937, the film received an Oscar nomination at the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences for Best Song: Whispers in the Dark, sung by Connee Boswell with Andre Kostelanetz and His Orchestra.
