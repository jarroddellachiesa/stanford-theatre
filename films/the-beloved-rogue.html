The Beloved Rogue

1927 ★ 99 min
Starring: John Barrymore, Angelo Rossitto, Conrad Veidt
Director: Alan Crosland
Writer: Paul Bern
Producer: Joseph M. Schenck

The Beloved Rogue is a 1927 American silent romantic adventure film, loosely based on the life of the 15th century French poet, François Villon. The film was directed by Alan Crosland for United Artists. François Villon is played by John Barrymore, and other cast members include Conrad Veidt as King Louis XI and Marceline Day as Charlotte de Vauxcelles.
