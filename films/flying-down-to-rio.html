Flying Down to Rio

1933 ★ 89 min
Starring: Gene Raymond, Dolores del Río, Fred Astaire
Director: Thornton Freeland
Writer: Cyril Hume, Adele Comandini, Erwin S. Gelsey
Producer: Merian C. Cooper

Flying Down to Rio is a 1933 American pre-Code musical film most famous for being the first screen pairing of Fred Astaire and Ginger Rogers, although lead actors Dolores del Río and Gene Raymond received top billing. Among the featured players are Franklin Pangborn and Eric Blore.
