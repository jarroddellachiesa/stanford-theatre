Road to Morocco

1942 ★ 83 min
Starring: Bing Crosby, Bob Hope, Dorothy Lamour
Director: David Butler
Writer: Don Hartman, Frank Butler
Producer: Paul Jones

Road to Morocco is a 1942 American comedy film starring Bing Crosby, Bob Hope and Dorothy Lamour, and featuring Anthony Quinn and Dona Drake. Written by Frank Butler and Don Hartman and directed by David Butler, it's the third of the "Road to ..." films. It was preceded by Road to Zanzibar (1941) and followed by Road to Utopia (1946).

Trivia

  • Nominated for: Academy Award for Best Writing, Original Screenplay; Academy Award for Best Sound.
