The Stanford Theatre Foundation and Cinequest Film Festival present
Silent Film Nights at San Jose's historic California Theatre

Friday March 3, 7:30 PM: Metropolis (1927) with Clark Wilson at the Wurlitzer organ
Friday March 10, 7:30 PM: Buster Keaton stars in Seven Chances (1925) and One Week (1920) with Christian Elliott at the Wurlitzer organ

Tickets are $10 and may be purchased online at www.cinequest.org or at the California Theatre on the day of the show.
The California Theatre is located at 345 South First Street in downtown San Jose, CA.
