Summer 2022

July 9 - September 16, 2022

Thursday Friday Saturday Sunday

On March 1, 2020, we were one of the first theatres to close because of the pandemic, and we have not rushed to reopen. We have made some changes to the ventilation system that should reduce the chance of infection, though nothing can eliminate all risk. But life cannot stop forever, and we have decided it is time to reopen. Masks are optional, but recommended.
