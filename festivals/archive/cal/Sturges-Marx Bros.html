The Stanford Theatre Foundation
presents silent film
at San Jose's historic California Theatre

March 4, 7:30 PM: Harold Lloyd in Safety Last (1923), with Chris Elliott at the organ.
Harold Lloyd's granddaughter Suzanne will appear at this screening.
2nd (sound) feature is Movie Crazy (1932)

March 11, 7:30 PM: Carmen (1915) and La Boheme (1926), with Dennis James at the organ

Tickets are available at www.cinequest.org, or by calling (408) 295-3378.
Tickets are also available at the Stanford Theatre box office Friday through Sundays during regular showtimes.

Please note that the silent films listed above are screening at the California Theatre in downtown San Jose, NOT at the Stanford Theatre. The California Theatre is located at 345 South First Street, San Jose.
